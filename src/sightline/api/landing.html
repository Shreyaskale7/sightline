Method

Four gates between a question and an answer.

Nothing ships on confidence alone. A question is routed, retrieved, answered, and then audited — and the audit can veto everything before it.

Gate 1 · Route

Classify the question’s shape.

A comparison fans out one search per company; a trend, one per quarter. The router picks the retrieval strategy that measured best for that shape — it’s a quality decision, not just a cost one.

Gate 2 · Retrieve

Find the pages, not just the words.

Sightline narrows 1,329 pages to the five most likely to hold the answer — filtering by company and filing type first, then re-reading each candidate page against your exact question before it makes the cut.

Gate 3 · Answer

Grounded on those pages only.

The model answers from the retrieved pages and must tag every claim with the page it came from. Outside knowledge is off the table.

Gate 4 · Audit

Uncited claims don’t ship.

A deterministic verifier strips citations to pages that were never retrieved and converts unsupported answers into refusals. ✓ verified answers get the page image, figure boxed. ✕ everything else is returned without answer.

Accuracy

Measured, not marketed.

✕

The feature is knowing when to say nothing.

Ask about a company that isn’t in the corpus, a forecast no filing contains, a fact that was never disclosed — and Sightline returns the question without an answer, in red ink, rather than improvising one. Abstention isn’t a failure state. It’s the audit working.
